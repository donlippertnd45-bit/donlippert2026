About Don

A Neighbor. A Worker. A Fighter for all North Dakotans.

Don Lippert brings a rare combination of backgrounds to this race — spanning Social Studies education, operations analytics, and hands-on industrial assembly work. That breadth of work experience isn't just a resume; it's a lens. Don understands North Dakota's workforce challenges from multiple vantage points: the classroom, the shop floor, and the data behind economic decisions.


His path to public service grew from a straightforward observation: North Dakota has many economic opportunities, but those opportunities are all too often out of reach for many North Dakotans who lack the resources necessary to take advantage of those industries. While the State has the ability to close some of those gaps, North Dakota's current legislators have lacked the political will to address these issues head on. He's running for the District 45 House seat to change that.

The Platform

Three Pillars for North Dakota's Future

Don's legislative agenda is built around the realities facing not only the families in District 45, but North Dakota's broader economy as well — not as talking points, but as reality.

💰
Economic Affordability

Fighting rising costs of housing, healthcare, and childcare for North Dakota families. Don believes economic security shouldn't depend on your zip code. He'll advocate for policy that makes life in North Dakota more affordable for the working and middle class.

🎓
Workforce & Education Investment

Expanding career training pathways, apprenticeship programs, and vocational training pipelines that connect North Dakota students directly to high-demand careers — including innovative ideas to bridge the urban-rural divide.

⚙️
Labor Policy & Worker Investment

Championing policies that treat workers as the backbone of North Dakota's economy. Don will advocate for fair wages, safe working conditions, and meaningful investment in the people who keep our communities and industries running.
